Cutoff for random walks on dihedral groups

Xiangying Huang and Renyu Rao. Submitted October 22, 2025.

Brief. We prove high-probability cutoff for random walks on finite dihedral groups driven by random generators, characterize the phase-dependent cutoff time, and develop entropy-concentration estimates for auxiliary high-dimensional lattice processes with dependent coordinates.

2026

Phenobarbital versus benzodiazepine-only treatment for alcohol withdrawal in the Emergency Department

Consulting report for UNC Emergency Department collaborators. This retrospective analysis of 1,267 emergency department encounters compares phenobarbital and benzodiazepine-only treatment strategies, using descriptive analyses, regression models, LASSO, and Elastic Net to evaluate associations with disposition and clinical outcomes.

2026

Evaluation of treatments for Temporomandibular Disorder, with and without comorbid headache

Consulting report for a UNC dentistry collaborator. This retrospective analysis of 70 patients evaluates treatment response in TMD and headache-associated TMD groups using paired t-tests, percent change in pain intensity, and regression adjustment for age and sex.

2022

Nonparametric maximum likelihood estimation for Gaussian mixture models

Selective mentee in Columbia University's M.A. Mentored Research program, supervised by Prof. Bodhisattva Sen. Studied location mixture models, optimizer existence, and a representation derived from Caratheodory's theorem on the likelihood vector space.

2021

Benford's Law of digit bias in simulated election data

PolyMath Summer Research Program participant. Worked in a five-person group with Prof. Steven Miller on theory and applications of Benford's Law, including R simulations of Weibull-distributed precinct-level election profiles and chi-square tests for fraud detection behavior.
