Applied Biostatistics Report

Evaluation of Treatments for Temporomandibular Disorder, with and without Comorbid Headache

Consulting report for UNC dentistry collaborator Ahmed Elgamal, analyzing treatment response among TMD patients with and without comorbid headache.

Read PDF Research Reports

Dataset

Retrospective dataset of 70 patients with Temporomandibular Disorder, including patients with and without comorbid headache.

Methods

Paired t-tests, percent change in pain intensity, and linear regression models adjusting for age and sex were used to evaluate treatment response.

Findings

Both groups showed pain reduction over time, with no statistically significant difference in treatment effectiveness between TMD and headache-associated TMD groups in this sample.
