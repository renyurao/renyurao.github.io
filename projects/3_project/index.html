PolyMath Summer Research Program

Benford's Law Simulations

In a five-person group with Prof. Steven Miller, I studied theory and applications of Benford's Law of digit bias.

Simulation

Explored simulation in R and generated Weibull-distributed precinct-level election profiles.

Testing

Applied Benford's Law and chi-square tests to simulated voting data.

Outcome

Studied fraud-detection sensitivity behavior in a controlled simulation setting.
