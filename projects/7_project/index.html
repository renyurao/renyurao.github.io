Applied Biostatistics Report

Phenobarbital versus Benzodiazepine-only Treatment for Alcohol Withdrawal in the Emergency Department

Consulting report for UNC Emergency Department collaborators Abby Opsal and Madison Mclemore, analyzing retrospective emergency department encounters for alcohol withdrawal.

Read PDF Research Reports

Dataset

Retrospective cohort of 1,267 emergency department encounters for alcohol withdrawal from June 2020 to July 2025.

Methods

Descriptive analyses, regression models, LASSO, and Elastic Net were used to study associations between treatment, dosing, disposition, and symptom trajectories.

Findings

Treatment group, cumulative benzodiazepine dose, and route of administration were strong predictors of disposition; findings are interpreted as associations rather than causal effects.
