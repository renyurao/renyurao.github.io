arXiv:2510.19942

Cutoff for Random Walks on Dihedral Groups

Joint work with Prof. Xiangying Huang on cutoff for random walks on finite dihedral groups and related random Cayley graph models.

Read PDF arXiv Page Publication Entry

Problem

Analyze continuous-time Markov chains on random Cayley graphs of finite dihedral groups generated by k independent random elements.

Main contribution

Prove high-probability cutoff in nearly the full regime 1 << log k << log |G| and characterize the phase-dependent cutoff time.

Techniques

Develop entropy-concentration estimates for auxiliary high-dimensional lattice processes with dependent coordinates, extending high-generator cutoff results to virtually Abelian groups.
